PEOPLE ASK

FrequentQuestions

FrequentQuestions

1. What topics can I cover in a 1-on-1 strategy call?

You can choose to cover UI/UX design skills, Figma/Webflow workflows, Upwork & Toptal profile optimization, client pricing, or moving to Germany (study visas, job seeker paths, and legal business setup).

2. Who is this mentorship suitable for?

Whether you are a beginner wanting to learn Product UI/UX, an intermediate freelancer looking to scale on Upwork or Toptal, or a professional planning to relocate and build a life in Germany, the sessions are customized to your need.

3. How do the Group Cohorts & Masterclasses work?

Group cohorts run in structured, batch-based formats. They feature live weekly interactive classes, hands-on design assignments, live portfolio reviews, and direct Q&A sessions in a private community.

4. How do I get feedback on my portfolio or profile?

You will get honest, actionable, line-by-line feedback through live meet up on your design portfolio, Case studies, Upwork proposals, CVs, or motivation letters for universities.

5. How can you guide me to relocate in Germany?

Having lived, studied, and built digital businesses in Germany for over 6 years, I provide realistic, step-by-step guidance on university admissions, job seeker visas, work permits, and settling down.

6. How do I book a 1-on-1 session with you?

Simply click the "Meet 1-on-1" button below, select a date and time that fits your schedule on Calendly, and submit your primary goals for the call so we can jump straight into strategy.
